=== PAGE 3 ===
Welcome to Lala Infotech. This handbook explains the rules every employee should know. It is a fictional sample made for learning. If something here is unclear, ask your HR partner.

Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Teams that support customers may work in shifts. Your manager shares the shift roster every Friday for the next week.

Please swipe your ID card when you enter and leave the office. Lost ID cards must be reported to the admin desk on the same day.

=== PAGE 7 ===
Work from home is allowed for 2 days a week after your probation ends. Ask your manager on the team calendar at least one day before.

Your laptop belongs to the company. Do not install software that is not on the approved list. Do not copy company files to personal drives or personal email.

If your laptop is slow or broken, raise a ticket with the IT helpdesk. Do not try to open the laptop yourself.

=== PAGE 12 ===
Leave policy. Every permanent employee gets 18 din of paid chhutti in a calendar year. Paid chhutti starts from the first day of joining and is counted month by month.

Unused paid chhutti up to 6 days can be carried to the next year. Anything above 6 days lapses on 31 December.

Sick leave is separate from paid chhutti. You get 8 days of sick leave in a year. For more than 2 sick days in a row, share a doctor's note with HR.

=== PAGE 15 ===
Holidays. The company publishes a list of 10 public holidays every December for the next year. You may also pick 2 optional holidays from the festival list.

If you have to work on a public holiday because of a customer issue, you get one compensatory day off. Take it within 30 days.

=== PAGE 21 ===
Travel and claims. Book office travel only through the travel desk. Keep every bill. Submit your claim within 15 days of coming back.

Food during office travel is covered up to the daily limit shown on the travel desk page. Alcohol is never covered.

Claims without bills are not paid. Your manager approves first, then finance.

=== PAGE 26 ===
Appraisal. Appraisals happen once a year in April. You fill a self review form, then your manager adds a review.

The appraisal looks at your work, your learning and feedback from your team. Write real examples of work you did in your self review.
