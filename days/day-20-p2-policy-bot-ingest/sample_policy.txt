=== PAGE 1 ===
Lala Infotech HR Policy (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)
This is a made up policy for learning only. Lala Infotech is not a real company.
Purpose: this document explains the basic rules for employees.
For questions, write to the HR helpdesk. Please read the policy before asking.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Working hours
Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
Lunch break is 45 minutes. Tea breaks are short and flexible.
Late arrival three times in a month needs a short note to your manager.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Types of leave
Employees get casual leave, sick leave and earned leave.
Casual leave: 8 days per year. Earned leave: 15 days per year.
Unused earned leave can be carried to the next year, up to 30 days.

=== PAGE 4 ===
Work from home (WFH)
Employees can take up to 2 WFH days per week.
WFH must be marked in the HR portal one day before.
On WFH days you must be online in office hours and reachable on chat.
New joiners can take WFH only after their first month.

=== PAGE 5 ===
Leave approval
Apply for casual leave at least 2 days before.
Your manager approves leave in the HR portal.
Leave for more than 5 days in a row needs approval 2 weeks before.

=== PAGE 6 ===
Holidays
The office follows a list of 10 public holidays per year.
The holiday list is shared in January.
If you work on a holiday, you get one compensatory off.

=== PAGE 7 ===
Sick leave
Employees get 7 days of sick leave per year.
For sick leave of more than 2 days in a row, a doctor's note is needed.
Inform your manager before 10 AM on the day of sick leave.

=== PAGE 8 ===
Travel
Office travel must be approved by your manager before booking.
Keep all bills. Submit travel claims within 15 days of return.

=== PAGE 9 ===
Laptop and IT
Use only the office laptop for office work.
Do not install unknown software. Raise a ticket with IT support for help.
Report a lost laptop to IT and HR on the same day.

=== PAGE 10 ===
Code of conduct
Treat every colleague with respect.
Do not share client data outside the company.
Report any problem to HR. Complaints are kept private.

=== PAGE 11 ===
Resignation and notice period
The notice period is 60 days.
Unused earned leave may be adjusted against the notice period with approval.

=== PAGE 12 ===
Policy changes and contacts
HR can update this policy. Changes are announced by email.
HR helpdesk: hr-helpdesk@lala-infotech.example
Salary review: please talk to your manager.
