Lala Infotech HR Policy (fictional sample data for learning, not a real company)

Annual leave: every full-time employee gets 18 days of paid leave in a calendar year. Unused paid leave does not carry over to the next year.

Sick leave: employees get 6 sick leave days per year. Tell your manager before 10 AM on the day you are unwell.

Working hours: the office works from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Lunch break is one hour.

Work from home: after probation, employees can work from home 2 days a week with manager approval.

Laptop: IT gives your laptop on your first working day. Do not install personal software on it.

ID card: wear your ID card in the office at all times. If you lose it, email the admin team the same day to get a new one.

Probation: the probation period is 6 months. Your manager reviews your work at the end of it.

Travel reimbursement: submit travel bills in the expense portal within 30 days of the trip. Attach every bill as a photo.

Notice period: after probation, the notice period is 60 days.
